What's in it for you

Give hours, get back a whole lot more

Volunteering shouldn't feel like a sacrifice. It should feel like the most alive part of your week.

Real skills, real fast

Lead a build site, run a distribution hub, manage a 40-person roster. Field experience no webinar can fake — plus a verified Stackly skill passport.

Your kind of people

Meet the ones who show up. 87% of volunteers tell us they found lasting friendships — and 214 found co-founders, partners or their next job through a shift.

Shifts that fit

Two hours or two weeks. Evenings, weekends, remote micro-tasks or full expedition builds — pick your pace in the app and swap shifts anytime.

Travel grants

Commit to 120 hours and Stackly covers flights and lodging on international builds. We fund the journey; you fund the grit.
